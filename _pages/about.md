### 🔍 Research Focus

I integrate advanced economic modeling with geospatial tools to address critical bottlenecks in the forest supply chain:

- **Forest Resource Economics & Bioeconomy**: Assessing market competitiveness, feedstock availability, and regional economic impacts of mass timber production.
- **Public Land Management & Policy**: Evaluating the economic viability of restoration thinning and the socio-economic effects of wildfire risk reduction treatments.
- **Spatial & Economic Modeling**: Utilizing **IMPLAN** for regional economic impact analysis and **ArcGIS Pro** for service area and facility location modeling.
