## Summary

This study develops precise local volume equations and mathematical form factors for *Shorea robusta* (Sal) trees across the lowland ecosystems of Western Nepal. These models serve as essential tools for local forest managers to predict timber yield, calculate standing tree biomass, and quantify regional forest carbon budgets accurately.

### Core Dimensions of the Study:
* **Allometric Modeling:** Establishing a high-accuracy predictor using a combination of tree diameter at breast height (DBH) and total height measurements to build reliable equations:

$$\ln(V) = a + b \ln(\text{DBH}) + c \ln(H)$$

* **Form Factor Calibration:** Demonstrating that standard government-prescribed form factors systematically vary from site-specific physical parameters.
* **Carbon Budget Applications:** Providing accurate baseline calculators essential for verifying national forest inventory metrics and carbon sequestration data.
