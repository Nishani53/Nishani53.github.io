## Summary

This research, funded by WWF-UK as part of the international Biome Health Project, evaluates how the dataset size, labeling accuracy, and image quality of camera-trap datasets impact the performance of deep learning computer vision algorithms used to automate wildlife population monitoring.

### Core Dimensions of the Study:
* **Computer Vision in Ecology:** Testing the limits of automated species identification models when dealing with complex, dense tropical forest background noise.
* **Data Quality vs. Quantity:** Analyzing whether massive, crowdsourced datasets out-perform smaller, highly precise expert-verified ecological datasets when training neural networks.
* **Collaborative Fieldwork:** Built upon field data collection infrastructure involving researchers, local field ecologists, and students from Tribhuvan University, Nepal.
