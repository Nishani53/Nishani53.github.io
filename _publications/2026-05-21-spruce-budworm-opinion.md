This opinion article examines Maine’s proactive efforts to mitigate the risks of another severe spruce budworm outbreak and highlights the ecological and economic importance of forest health management.

## Summary

The article discusses historical impacts of spruce budworm outbreaks in Maine and explores current monitoring, management, and policy approaches aimed at reducing future forest damage.

### Key Discussion Points:
* **Forest Health Risks:** The ecological and economic consequences of widespread spruce budworm infestations.
* **Early Detection and Monitoring:** Ongoing surveillance and scientific efforts to identify outbreak risks before large-scale damage occurs.
* **Economic Importance:** Potential impacts on Maine’s forest products industry and rural communities.
* **Policy and Preparedness:** The role of coordinated forest management strategies in preventing future outbreaks.
