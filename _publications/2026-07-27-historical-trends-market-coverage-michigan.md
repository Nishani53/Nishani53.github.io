## Abstract
This study examines historical trends in market coverage, competition hotspots, and merchantable volume for hardwood and softwood sawlogs, pulpwood, and biomass chips and residues from 1985 to 2023. Hardwood and softwood sawlog markets expanded over the study period, with notable increases between 2003 and 2018. Although the total number of sawlog processors declined, capacity became increasingly concentrated in the largest mills enabling production to remain stable.

## Key Findings
* **Sawlog Market Expansion:** Both hardwood and softwood sawlog markets expanded significantly, with processing capacity concentrating in larger facilities near population centers.
* **Pulpwood Trends & Dynamics:** Market coverage doubled over time, though declining mill numbers weakened local markets despite recent entries of large processing firms.
* **Biomass Market Growth:** Biomass market coverage rose sharply from 0.48 million acres in 1985 to 15.53 million acres in 2018, with merchantable volume doubling over that span.
* **Policy Implications:** Highlights the need for targeted policies to sustain rural wood product markets and support private landowners with limited buyer competition.
